About DocAura

DocAura is a set of free tools for everyday paperwork: making a resume, shrinking a PDF for an online form, resizing a photo to 50KB, or making a passport photo.

Why we built it

Too many “free” websites ask you to sign up, add a watermark, or ask for payment at the very last step. Many are also hard to use if you are not comfortable with technology. We wanted tools that are genuinely free and simple enough for anyone — students, job seekers, parents and grandparents.

Your files stay with you

Every tool runs inside your own web browser. Your photos, PDFs and resume details are never uploaded to our servers — we never see them.

How we keep it free

DocAura shows a small number of advertisements. They pay for the website so the tools can stay free for everyone.

Get in touch

Found a problem or want a new tool? Email hello [at] docaura [dot] online or use the contact page. We read every message and usually reply within 2 days.

DocAura is made in India and runs at docaura.online. No account is needed for any free tool, and we never ask for passwords or OTPs.
