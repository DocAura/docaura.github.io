3See your ageExact years, months and days appear instantly. Add the age limit to check eligibility.

Questions people ask

What does “age as on 1 August” mean?

Exams fix a cut-off date. Your age is counted on that date, not today. Put the cut-off date in the “As on date” box to see the age the exam will use.

How is the age limit relaxation counted?

Usually OBC gets 3 extra years, SC/ST 5 years, and PwBD 10 years (more when combined with a category). Rules differ per exam, so always confirm in the official notification.

Is this calculation exact?

Yes. It counts full years, then full months, then the remaining days, the same way exam boards do.

Can I use it for any date, not only exams?

Yes — retirement, school admission, pension or just for fun. Change the “As on date” to any date.
