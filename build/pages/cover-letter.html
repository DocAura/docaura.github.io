Cover letter tips

1Keep it to one pageThree or four short paragraphs are enough. Hiring managers skim.
2Show, don’t tellPick one or two real results with numbers instead of listing qualities.
3Make it about themSay why you want this company — mention something specific you like about it.

Questions people ask

Is the cover letter builder free?

Yes — no sign-up, no watermark and no payment. Download as many letters as you like.

Do I need to make a resume first?

No, but it helps. If you have made a resume here, we use it to write your first draft and match its design. Otherwise just fill in your details.

Should I use “Yours sincerely” or “Yours faithfully”?

In the UK, use “Yours sincerely” when you know the person’s name and “Yours faithfully” when you start with “Dear Hiring Manager”. In the US and most other places, “Sincerely” or “Kind regards” is fine.
