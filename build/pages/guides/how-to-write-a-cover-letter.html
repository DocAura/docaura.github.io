A cover letter is a short letter sent with your resume. It tells the employer why you want this job and why you are a good fit — in your own words. A good one is short, specific and about the employer, not just about you.

The four-paragraph structure

1. Opening: the job and why you fit

Name the job you are applying for and give one sentence on why you are a strong match — your experience and two or three key skills from the job advert.

2. Your best achievements

Pick one or two real results, ideally with numbers, that prove you can do this job. Don’t repeat your whole resume.

3. Why this company

Say something specific you like about the company — its products, customers, values or growth — and how you would help.

4. Closing

Thank them, say you would like to talk, and sign off politely.

Which greeting and sign-off?

SituationGreetingSign-off
You know the person’s name (UK)Dear Ms Patel,Yours sincerely,
You don’t know the name (UK)Dear Hiring Manager,Yours faithfully,
USA and most other placesDear Ms Patel, / Dear Hiring Manager,Sincerely, / Kind regards,

Mistakes to avoid

  • Too long. Keep it to one page — three or four short paragraphs.
  • Generic. “I am a hard-working team player” could be anyone. Use a real example instead.
  • Wrong company name. When you reuse a letter, double-check every name.
  • All about you. Show how you will help them.
  • Typos. Read it aloud before sending.
