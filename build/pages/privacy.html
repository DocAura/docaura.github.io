Privacy policy

Last updated: {{UPDATED}}

The short version

Your files never leave your device. All tools on {{BRAND}} work inside your own web browser. We do not upload, store or see your photos, PDFs or resume details.

Information stored on your device

The resume builder saves what you type in your browser’s local storage so you don’t lose your work. The “Bigger text” setting is saved the same way. This information stays on your device; you can delete it by clearing your browser data or pressing “Start empty” in the resume builder.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, or learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. Where required by law, you will be asked for consent before such cookies are used.

Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics to count page visits (for example, which tools are most popular). This never includes the content of your files.

Fonts and hosting

Our pages load the Poppins font from Google Fonts and are hosted on GitHub Pages. These services may receive your IP address as part of normal web requests.

Children

{{BRAND}} is a general-audience website and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
