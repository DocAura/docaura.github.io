    Questions people ask

    How do I make a PDF smaller than 200KB?

    Choose your PDF above — 200KB is already selected — and press the big button. You’ll see the new size before you download.

    Is 200KB the same as 0.2MB?

    Almost. 200KB is about 0.2MB. Our result always stays under the limit you choose, so it is safe for a “max 200KB” form.

    Can I compress a scanned PDF to 200KB?

    Yes. For scans, also tick “Black & white” — it gives a cleaner, smaller file.

    Is it safe for my documents?

    Yes. Your PDF is processed inside your own browser and is never uploaded to any server.
