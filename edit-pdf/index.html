Questions people ask

How do I add text to a PDF?

Choose “Text”, tap where you want to write, and type. Use A− and A+ to change the size, and drag the round handle to move it.

Can I change the text that is already in the PDF?

You can cover old text with “White-out” and type new text on top. Changing the original words directly is not possible for most PDFs, because PDFs store text like a picture of letters.

Can I type in Hindi, Arabic or Chinese?

Yes. Text in any language is kept exactly as you typed it.

Is it free and private?

Yes. No sign-up, no watermark, no limit. Your PDF is edited inside your browser and never uploaded.
