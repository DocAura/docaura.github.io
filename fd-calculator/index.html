Questions people ask

How do banks calculate FD interest?

Most Indian banks compound FD interest every quarter. Maturity = P × (1 + r/400)^(4 × years). Choose “Quarterly” for bank FDs.

Is FD interest taxable?

Yes. FD interest is added to your income and taxed at your slab rate. Banks deduct TDS if interest crosses the yearly limit; submit Form 15G/15H if eligible.

Do senior citizens get more?

Usually 0.25%–0.50% extra. Type your bank’s senior citizen rate in the rate box.
