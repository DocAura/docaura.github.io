Questions people ask

How do I fill a PDF form?

Choose the form. If it has fillable boxes, they turn light blue — tap them and type. If not, choose “Text” and tap anywhere to type.

Can I tick boxes?

Yes. Fillable checkboxes can be ticked directly, and on any form you can use “Tick” or “Cross” and tap the box.

What does “Lock the answers” do?

It turns your answers into a normal part of the page, so nobody can change them later. Leave it off if you may need to edit the form again.

Can I sign the form too?

Yes — after saving, open “Sign a PDF” to add your signature, or add a picture of your signature with “Picture”.
