Bank exam photo, signature and declaration size (IBPS, SBI, RRB)

Bank exam forms (IBPS PO, Clerk, RRB, SO and many SBI forms) ask for four uploads. The usual sizes are below — but check your notification, because each recruitment can change them.

Quick reference

  • Photograph: 200 × 230 pixels, 20KB – 50KB, JPG, recent, light background.
  • Signature: 140 × 60 pixels, 10KB – 20KB, black ink on white paper.
  • Left thumb impression: black or blue ink on white paper, JPG. The pixel and KB size differ between notifications — read yours.
  • Handwritten declaration: usually 50KB – 100KB JPG, written by you in black ink on white paper, word for word as given in the notification.

Make each file in one minute

  1. Photo: open Resize photo to KB, choose 50KB, turn on “Also set exact size in pixels” and type 200 × 230. Need a white background first? Use Make background white.
  2. Signature: open Signature resize. “140 × 60 (Bank, IBPS, SBI)” and “10KB – 20KB” are already selected — just choose your signature and download.
  3. Thumb impression and declaration: scan them on white paper with Resize photo to KB using Custom size, or turn them into a PDF with Photos to PDF if the form wants a PDF.

Tips that prevent rejection

  • Use the same photo you will carry on exam day — your face is matched at the exam centre.
  • Don’t sign in CAPITAL letters — most banks reject that.
  • Write the declaration in your own handwriting — not typed, not by someone else.
  • Check your age limit on the cut-off date with the Age calculator.
