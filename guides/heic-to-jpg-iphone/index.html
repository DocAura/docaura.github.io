You take a photo on your iPhone, try to upload it to a form or open it on a Windows computer — and it doesn’t work. The reason is usually the file type: HEIC.

What is HEIC?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the photo format iPhones use by default. It keeps good quality at about half the file size of a JPG, which saves space on your phone. The problem is that many websites, upload forms and older computers only accept JPG or PNG.

HEIC vs JPG at a glance

Make your iPhone take JPG photos instead

If you often need JPGs, change the camera setting:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Camera, then Formats.
  3. Choose Most Compatible.

This only affects new photos. Photos you already took stay HEIC — convert them with the tool above. The downside of “Most Compatible” is that photos take up more space on your phone.

Sending photos to a Windows PC?

On your iPhone, go to Settings → Photos and look for “Transfer to Mac or PC”. Setting it to Automatic lets the iPhone convert photos to a compatible format when you copy them to a computer with a cable. (On some iOS versions the Photos settings are under Settings → Apps → Photos.)
