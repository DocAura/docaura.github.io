Resume action words to replace “responsible for”

“Responsible for…”, “Worked on…”, “Helped with…” — these phrases tell an employer what your duties were, not what you achieved. Starting each line with a strong action word, and adding a number, makes your resume clearer and more impressive in seconds.

The simple formula

Action word + what you did + the result (with a number)
“Reduced customer waiting time by 30% by reorganising the front desk schedule.”

Before and after

BeforeAfter
Responsible for managing social mediaGrew Instagram followers from 2,000 to 15,000 in one year
Worked on the billing systemBuilt a billing feature used by 40,000 customers a month
Helped with customer complaintsResolved 25+ customer complaints a day with a 95% satisfaction score
In charge of new staffTrained 12 new team members in their first month

Action words by skill

Leadership

Led, Managed, Directed, Supervised, Coordinated, Mentored, Coached, Guided, Headed, Oversaw, Delegated, Motivated, Organised, Chaired, Spearheaded

Achievement and results

Achieved, Increased, Improved, Reduced, Saved, Exceeded, Grew, Boosted, Won, Delivered, Completed, Accelerated, Expanded, Doubled, Cut

Building and creating

Built, Created, Designed, Developed, Launched, Introduced, Established, Founded, Produced, Wrote, Set up, Started, Invented, Prototyped, Shaped

Communication

Presented, Explained, Negotiated, Persuaded, Wrote, Edited, Published, Promoted, Represented, Collaborated, Partnered, Advised, Consulted, Answered, Pitched

Problem solving and analysis

Analysed, Solved, Researched, Investigated, Identified, Diagnosed, Evaluated, Tested, Measured, Calculated, Forecast, Planned, Streamlined, Simplified, Fixed

Helping people and customers

Supported, Served, Assisted, Cared for, Resolved, Advocated, Welcomed, Guided, Counselled, Taught, Tutored, Volunteered, Responded, Helped, Comforted

Organising and admin

Organised, Scheduled, Arranged, Prepared, Processed, Recorded, Tracked, Maintained, Filed, Updated, Catalogued, Monitored, Ordered, Budgeted, Planned

Sales and money

Sold, Closed, Generated, Secured, Negotiated, Budgeted, Reconciled, Audited, Invoiced, Forecasted, Priced, Upsold, Retained, Converted, Prospected

Three rules

  1. Use past tense for past jobs (“Managed”) and present tense for your current job (“Manage”).
  2. Don’t repeat the same word on every line — mix them up.
  3. Be honest. Only claim results you really achieved; interviewers will ask about them.
