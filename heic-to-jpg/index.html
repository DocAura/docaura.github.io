Questions people ask

What is a HEIC file?

HEIC is the photo format iPhones use to save space. Many websites, forms and Windows computers cannot open it, so converting to JPG fixes that.

Can I convert many photos at once?

Yes. Choose as many HEIC photos as you like and they are converted one after another.

Is the quality kept?

Yes. Photos are saved as high-quality JPGs. If you need a smaller file, use our “Resize photo to KB” tool afterwards.
