    Questions people ask

    How do I convert a JPG to PDF?

    Choose one or more photos, set the order with the arrows, pick a page size and press “Make my PDF”.

    Can I add photos from my iPhone?

    Yes. iPhone HEIC photos are supported and are converted automatically.

    Which page size should I choose?

    Choose A4 if you will print it or upload it to a form. Choose “Same as photo” to keep each page the exact shape of the photo.

    The PDF is too big for my form. What now?

    After downloading, open our “Make PDF smaller” tool and choose the size your form allows.
