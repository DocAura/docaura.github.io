    How to scan homework into one PDF

    1Take clear photosLay the paper flat in good light, hold the phone straight above it, and take one photo per page.
    2Fix and orderChoose “Clear document”, turn any sideways pages, and use the arrows to put pages in order.
    3Check and uploadLook at the preview, check the page count, then upload the single PDF.

    Questions people ask

    My photos are dark or have shadows. Can this fix them?

    Yes. Choose “Clear document” to remove shadows and whiten the paper, or “Black & white” for the sharpest, smallest file. You can see the result on each page before you download.

    How do I turn a sideways page?

    Tap the turn button next to that page. Each tap turns it a quarter turn.

    My school only accepts files under 5MB or 10MB.

    Pick “Under 5MB” or “Under 10MB” and the tool automatically keeps the PDF below that size.

    Can I add photos from my iPhone?

    Yes. iPhone HEIC photos are supported and converted automatically.

    Which page size should I choose?

    A4 for most of the world, US Letter for the USA and Canada. “Same as photo” keeps each page the exact shape of the photo.
