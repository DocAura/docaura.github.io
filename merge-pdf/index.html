    Questions people ask

    How do I merge PDF files?

    Choose all your PDFs (you can pick many at once), use the up and down arrows to set the order, then press “Join my PDFs”.

    Can I take only some pages from each file?

    Yes. Each PDF in the list has a “Pages” box. Type pages like 1-3, 5 to use only those, or leave it empty for the whole file.

    Can I add photos too?

    Yes. JPG, PNG and iPhone photos become full pages in the joined PDF.

    Is there a limit on files?

    There is no fixed limit. Very large files may be slow on older phones, because all the work happens on your device.

    Will the quality change?

    No. Pages are copied exactly as they are. Nothing is compressed.

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. Joining happens inside your browser, so your PDFs stay private.
