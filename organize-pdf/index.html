Questions people ask

How do I rotate one page of a PDF?

Tap the turn button under that page. Each tap turns it a quarter turn. Only that page changes.

How do I change the page order?

Use the left and right arrows under each page to move it earlier or later.

How do I delete a page?

Tap the bin button under the page. You can undo with “Start over” before saving.

Will the quality change?

No. Pages are kept exactly as they are; only their direction and order change.
