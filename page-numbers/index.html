    Questions people ask

    How do I add page numbers to a PDF?

    Choose your PDF, pick where the numbers go and how they look, then press “Add numbers”. Your original file is not changed.

    Can I add my name and roll number to every page?

    Yes. Type it in “Text on every page”, for example “Priya Sharma · Class 10B · Roll 23”, and it appears at the top of every page.

    Can I start from a number other than 1?

    Yes. Change “Start at” — useful when your file continues from an earlier part.

    Does it work on scanned PDFs?

    Yes. The numbers are added on top of each page, so it works with scans and photos too.
