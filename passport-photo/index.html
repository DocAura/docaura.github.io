Questions people ask

What size is a passport photo?

Many countries, including India, the UK and most of Europe, use 35 × 45 mm. The USA and many visa forms use 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm). Always check the rules on your own form.

How do I take a good passport photo at home?

Stand in front of a plain white or light wall, face the camera straight on, keep a neutral face with your mouth closed, and use soft daylight without shadows.

How do I print it?

Download the 4 × 6 inch sheet and print it as a normal 4 × 6 photo at any photo shop, or print the A4 sheet at home at “actual size” (100%).

My form needs the photo under 50KB.
