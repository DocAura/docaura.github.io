    Questions people ask

    How do I convert a PDF to JPG?

    Choose your PDF, pick the quality, and press “Make pictures”. Then save each page, or all of them at once.

    My browser asks to allow many downloads. Is that okay?

    Yes. When you save all pages at once, the browser asks once whether this site may download several files. Tap Allow.

    JPG or PNG — which should I pick?

    JPG makes smaller files and is best for most uses. PNG is sharper for pages with small text, but the files are bigger.
