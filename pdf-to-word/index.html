    Questions people ask

    How do I convert a PDF to Word?

    Choose your PDF and press “Convert to Word”. You get a .docx file that opens in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, WPS or LibreOffice.

    Will it look exactly like my PDF?

    The words, paragraphs and headings are kept so you can edit them easily. Fancy layouts — side-by-side columns, tables and pictures — are simplified into plain text.

    My PDF is a scan. Will this work?

    A scanned PDF is made of pictures, so it has no text to copy. The tool will tell you, and can put each page into Word as a picture instead.

    Is my document safe?

    Yes. The conversion happens inside your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to any server.
