About this tool

Many recruitment and entrance exams — including UPSC and several state commissions — ask that your photo shows your name and the date the photo was taken printed below it. This tool adds both in clear black capital letters on a white strip, keeps the photo in the 3.5 × 4.5 shape, and saves it under the KB size you choose.

Always use the date the photo was actually taken, and check your notification — some exams want the photo taken within the last 10 days or 3 months.

How to join photo and signature

1Choose your photoA clear, front-facing photo with a light background works best.
2Add signature or nameSign with a black pen on white paper and take a photo, or type your name and date.
3DownloadPick the KB size your form asks for and download one JPG.

Questions people ask

Which exams need name and date on the photo?

UPSC asks for it, and some state public service commissions and university exams do too. Check the “photograph” section of your notification.

Can I change the date format?

The date is printed as DD/MM/YYYY, the format most Indian forms use.

Are my photo and signature uploaded?

No. Everything happens inside your browser. Your pictures never leave your phone or computer.
