    Questions people ask

    How do I compress a JPG to 100KB?

    Choose your photo above. 100KB is already selected — press the big button and download.

    Will it change the photo size in pixels?

    Only if needed. We first lower the quality slightly, and make the photo smaller in pixels only when that isn’t enough.

    Can I do many photos?

    Yes — after one is done, press “Do another photo”.

    Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

    No. The photo is resized inside your own browser. It never leaves your phone or computer.
