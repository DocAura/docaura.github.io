    Where you need a photo under 200KB

    • Forms that accept photos up to 200KB
    • Phone photos that are 3–10MB
    • Product photos for online shops
    • Blog and website images

    Phone cameras make photos of 3–10MB. At 200KB they still look almost the same on a screen but upload many times faster. For printed passport photos, use Passport photo instead, which keeps full quality.

    Questions people ask

    How do I reduce a photo to 200KB?

    Choose your photo above. 200KB is already selected — press the big button and download.

    Will the quality drop?

    Very little. 200KB is enough for a clear photo at a normal screen size.

    Does it work with iPhone photos?

    Yes. iPhone HEIC photos are converted and saved as JPG.

    Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

    No. The photo is resized inside your own browser. It never leaves your phone or computer.
