    Questions people ask

    How do I compress an image to 30KB?

    Choose your photo above. 30KB is already set in the custom box — press the big button and download.

    Which format do I get?

    A JPG file, which almost every form accepts.

    Can I use a PNG or iPhone HEIC photo?

    Yes. We accept JPG, PNG, WEBP and iPhone HEIC photos and turn them into a JPG.

    Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

    No. The photo is resized inside your own browser. It never leaves your phone or computer.
