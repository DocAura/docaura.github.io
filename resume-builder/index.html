Resume tips that get interviews

1Start each line with an action“Managed”, “Built”, “Reduced”, “Trained”. It shows what you did, not just your duties.
2Add numbers“Handled 120 customer calls a day” is stronger than “Handled customer calls”.
3Match the job postUse the same skill words as the job advert. Many companies scan resumes for them.

Questions people ask

Is this resume builder really free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no payment at the end. You can download as many times as you like.

Is my information safe?

Your details are saved only in your own browser on this device. Nothing is sent to us or any server.

Does the job match give my official ATS score?

No tool can give an official score — every company’s system is different. Our check compares the words in the job advert with your resume, so you can see which important skills and keywords you may be missing.

Will my resume work with company job portals (ATS)?

Yes. The PDF contains real text, not a picture, in a simple one-column layout that job portals can read.

Can I write my resume in Hindi or another language?

Right now the PDF supports English and other languages written with Latin letters, including accents like é, ñ and ü. More scripts are coming soon.

My upload form needs the PDF under 100KB.

Resumes made here are usually well under 100KB. If needed, use our Make PDF smaller tool.
