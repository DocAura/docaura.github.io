Accountant resume summary examples

Your summary is the first thing people read. Keep it to 2–3 lines: who you are, your experience, and what you are best at.

Chartered accountant with 7 years of experience in GST, audits and month-end closing for manufacturing and retail companies. Known for clean books, on-time filings and training junior staff.
ACCA-qualified accountant with 5 years of experience in month-end close, VAT returns and management reporting for UK retail businesses.
Detail-focused junior accountant with 2 years of experience in accounts payable, bank reconciliations and payroll using QuickBooks and Excel.

Skills to put on a accountant resume

Add the ones you really have — and use the same words as the job advert.

  • Financial reporting
  • Month-end close
  • Reconciliations
  • Tax / GST / VAT filing
  • Audit support
  • Accounts payable & receivable
  • Payroll
  • Advanced Excel
  • Tally / QuickBooks / SAP
  • Budgeting
  • Forecasting
  • IFRS or GAAP

Accountant resume bullet points

Start with an action word and add a number to show the result. Copy these ideas and change them to match your own work:

  • Closed monthly books 3 days faster by building Excel templates for reconciliations
  • Filed GST returns for 4 business units with zero penalties in 3 years
  • Led a team of 3 junior accountants and trained them on Tally Prime
  • Handled accounts and tax filings for 40+ small business clients
  • Reduced month-end closing time from 8 to 5 days
  • Recovered $45,000 in duplicate supplier payments through a payables review
  • Prepared audit files that passed with no major findings for 3 years running
  • Built an Excel cash-flow forecast used by the CFO every week
  • Processed payroll for 250 employees with 100% on-time payment

Tips for your accountant resume

  1. Put your professional qualification (CA, CPA, ACCA, CMA) right after your name or in your title.
  2. Name the software you know — employers often filter by Tally, SAP, QuickBooks or Xero.
  3. Show size: number of clients, entities or the value of accounts you handled.

Questions people ask

What skills should an accountant put on a resume?

Reporting, reconciliations, tax filing, audit, Excel and the accounting software you use, plus soft skills like accuracy and meeting deadlines.

Should I include my CA or CPA exam results?

Include the qualification and year. Add ranks or distinctions if they are strong.

Which resume design is best for accountants?

A simple, classic design. Classic, Executive and Chronological are good choices — all are ATS-friendly.
