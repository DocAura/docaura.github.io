Why this administrative assistant resume works

  • Shows who you support (3 senior managers) and how.
  • Improvement with a number: half the time finding documents.
  • MS Office and typing speed are listed.
  • Covers both admin and reception experience.

Administrative Assistant resume summary examples

Your summary is the first thing people read. Keep it to 2–3 lines: who you are, your experience, and what you are best at.

Organised administrative assistant with 6 years of experience supporting managers and busy offices. Excellent with calendars, travel, documents and MS Office, and the friendly first contact for visitors.
Friendly receptionist with 4 years of experience in hotels and clinics, managing bookings, phones and visitors with a smile.
Office manager with 8 years of experience running day-to-day operations for a 60-person company, from facilities to HR admin.

Skills to put on a administrative assistant resume

Add the ones you really have — and use the same words as the job advert.

  • MS Office (Word, Excel, Outlook)
  • Calendar management
  • Travel booking
  • Data entry
  • Document management
  • Reception & phones
  • Invoicing
  • Minute taking
  • Typing speed
  • Confidentiality
  • Organisation
  • Communication

Administrative Assistant resume bullet points

Start with an action word and add a number to show the result. Copy these ideas and change them to match your own work:

  • Manage calendars, meetings and travel for 3 senior managers
  • Set up a shared filing system that cut time spent finding documents by half
  • Process 200+ invoices a month and track office supplies within budget
  • Welcomed 100+ visitors a day and answered a busy phone line
  • Organised 30+ meetings and events a month, including travel for visitors
  • Reduced stationery costs by 18% by switching suppliers
  • Prepared reports and presentations for weekly management meetings
  • Answered 80+ calls a day and directed them to the right team
  • Onboarded new staff with IDs, equipment and system access

Tips for your administrative assistant resume

  1. Show who you support and how much (people, meetings, invoices).
  2. Include typing speed and Office skills — they are often screened.
  3. Mention confidentiality if you handled sensitive documents.

Questions people ask

What skills are most important for an admin assistant?

Organisation, MS Office, communication, calendar and travel management, and attention to detail.

How is a receptionist resume different?

It focuses more on visitors, phones and bookings. Change the summary and bullets — the layout stays the same.

Should I include typing speed?

Yes, if it is good (50+ words per minute).
