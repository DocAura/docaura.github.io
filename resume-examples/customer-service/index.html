Customer service roles are about solving problems while keeping people happy. This customer service resume example shows the volume you handle, your satisfaction scores and the support tools you know.

Why this customer service resume works

  • Volume (60+ contacts a day) and satisfaction score (96%) are shown upfront.
  • Shows improvement, not just duties: handling time cut from 9 to 6 minutes.
  • Support tools like Zendesk and Salesforce are listed.
  • Languages are included — a big plus in support jobs.

Customer Service resume summary examples

Your summary is the first thing people read. Keep it to 2–3 lines: who you are, your experience, and what you are best at.

Friendly customer service representative with 5 years of experience on phone, chat and email support. I solve problems quickly, stay calm with upset customers and keep satisfaction scores high.
Patient call centre agent with 3 years of experience in banking support, handling account and card queries while following strict security rules.
Customer support specialist for a SaaS product, solving technical questions by chat and email and writing help-centre articles.

Skills to put on a customer service resume

Add the ones you really have — and use the same words as the job advert.

  • Customer support
  • Phone & live chat
  • Email support
  • Complaint resolution
  • Zendesk / Freshdesk
  • CRM software
  • Active listening
  • Empathy
  • Fast, accurate typing
  • Multitasking
  • Upselling
  • Problem solving

Customer Service resume bullet points

Start with an action word and add a number to show the result. Copy these ideas and change them to match your own work:

  • Handle 60+ customer calls and chats a day with a 96% satisfaction score
  • Cut average handling time from 9 to 6 minutes by writing quick-answer templates
  • Trained 8 new team members on billing and the Zendesk system
  • Answered order and refund questions by phone and email
  • Kept a 4.8/5 customer rating across 10,000+ conversations
  • Wrote 40 help-centre articles that cut repeat questions by 20%
  • Handled escalated complaints for the team lead
  • Met response-time targets 99% of the time
  • Upsold premium plans to 15% of callers

Tips for your customer service resume

  1. Show volume and quality together: contacts per day and satisfaction score.
  2. List every language you speak and the level.
  3. Name the support software you have used.

Questions people ask

What skills are best for a customer service resume?

Communication, problem solving, patience, typing speed, CRM/helpdesk tools and any languages you speak.

How do I describe a call centre job on a resume?

Say how many calls you handled, your quality or satisfaction scores, and any improvements you made.

Is one page enough?

Yes. For most customer service roles one page is ideal.
