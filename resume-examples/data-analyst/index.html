Data Analyst resume summary examples

Your summary is the first thing people read. Keep it to 2–3 lines: who you are, your experience, and what you are best at.

Data analyst with 4 years of experience turning sales and customer data into clear dashboards and decisions. Strong in SQL, Python and Power BI, and good at explaining numbers to non-technical teams.
Entry-level data analyst with a statistics degree and projects in SQL, Python and Tableau, analysing public datasets on health and transport.
Business analyst with 5 years of experience in retail, turning sales data into weekly insights and forecasting models for leadership.

Skills to put on a data analyst resume

Add the ones you really have — and use the same words as the job advert.

  • SQL
  • Python (Pandas, NumPy)
  • Excel (Pivot tables, VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP)
  • Power BI
  • Tableau
  • Statistics
  • Data cleaning
  • Data visualisation
  • A/B testing
  • Forecasting
  • Google BigQuery
  • Storytelling with data

Data Analyst resume bullet points

Start with an action word and add a number to show the result. Copy these ideas and change them to match your own work:

  • Built Power BI sales dashboards used daily by 50+ managers
  • Found a checkout drop-off issue through funnel analysis that lifted conversions by 8%
  • Automated weekly reports with Python and SQL, saving 10 hours a week
  • Cleaned and analysed survey data from 20,000+ customers
  • Built a churn model that identified 70% of at-risk customers
  • Combined 5 data sources into one reporting database
  • Presented monthly insights to the leadership team
  • Saved 12 hours a week by automating Excel reports with Python
  • Improved data accuracy from 85% to 99% with validation checks

Tips for your data analyst resume

  1. Put your tools in the summary — screeners look for SQL, Python and BI tools first.
  2. Every bullet should end with the business result of your analysis.
  3. Add a portfolio link (GitHub, Tableau Public) if you are starting out.

Questions people ask

What should a data analyst resume include?

Tools (SQL, Python, Excel, BI), the types of data you worked with, and the business decisions your work supported.

Should I add projects?

Yes — especially if you are new. Describe the question, the data, the tools and the result.

Which design is best?

A simple, ATS-friendly one-column design: Scholar, Tech or Compact.
