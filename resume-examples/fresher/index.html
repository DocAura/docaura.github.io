Fresher resume example for students and graduates

Updated 20 October 2026 · Free to edit and download

When you have no full-time job yet, your education, projects, internships and skills do the talking. This fresher resume example puts education first and turns projects and internships into results an employer can trust.

Why this fresher / student resume works

  • Education is at the top, with marks/CGPA.
  • Internship and college project are written like real work, with numbers.
  • Skills match what entry-level tech jobs ask for.
  • Certificates and hackathon wins add proof.

Fresher / Student resume summary examples

Your summary is the first thing people read. Keep it to 2–3 lines: who you are, your experience, and what you are best at.

Final-year computer engineering student with hands-on project and internship experience in Python and web development. Quick learner looking for my first full-time role.
Recent B.Com graduate with strong Excel and Tally skills from an accounting internship. Eager to start my career in finance and learn quickly.
Final-year mechanical engineering student with hands-on experience from a 2-month plant internship and a college go-kart project.

Skills to put on a fresher / student resume

Add the ones you really have — and use the same words as the job advert.

  • MS Excel
  • Communication
  • Teamwork
  • Problem solving
  • Python / Java / C
  • SQL
  • HTML & CSS
  • Presentation
  • Time management
  • Quick learner

Fresher / Student resume bullet points

Start with an action word and add a number to show the result. Copy these ideas and change them to match your own work:

  • Built 3 internal web pages with React used by 40+ employees
  • Wrote Python scripts that cleaned 10,000 records of customer data
  • Led a team of 4 to build a face-recognition attendance app for 300 students
  • Built a college event website used by 1,200 students
  • Led a team of 4 in a final-year project that won 2nd prize
  • Completed a 2-month internship supporting the accounts team
  • Organised a coding workshop for 80 junior students
  • Volunteered weekly teaching computers to school children

Tips for your fresher / student resume

  1. Put Education first, with your degree, college, year and marks or CGPA.
  2. Treat projects, internships and volunteering like jobs — with bullets and numbers.
  3. Keep it to one page.

Questions people ask

What is the best resume format for freshers?

A one-page resume with Education at the top, then Projects / Internships, Skills and Certifications. The Fresher and Harvard designs are made for this.

Should a fresher add a photo?

It depends on the country. In India and the Middle East a photo is common; in the US and UK leave it out.

What if I have no internship?

Use college projects, volunteering, part-time work, clubs or online courses. See our guide for resumes with no experience.
