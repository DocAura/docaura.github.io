Graphic Designer resume summary examples

Your summary is the first thing people read. Keep it to 2–3 lines: who you are, your experience, and what you are best at.

Creative graphic designer with 5 years of experience in branding, social media and packaging design. I turn ideas into clean, memorable visuals and deliver on tight deadlines.
UI/UX designer with 3 years of experience designing mobile apps in Figma, from user research to high-fidelity prototypes.
Junior graphic designer and recent graduate with a strong portfolio in branding and illustration, ready to grow in an agency team.

Skills to put on a graphic designer resume

Add the ones you really have — and use the same words as the job advert.

  • Adobe Illustrator
  • Photoshop
  • InDesign
  • Figma
  • Branding
  • Typography
  • Layout
  • Social media design
  • Print production
  • Motion graphics (After Effects)
  • Colour theory
  • Client presentations

Graphic Designer resume bullet points

Start with an action word and add a number to show the result. Copy these ideas and change them to match your own work:

  • Designed brand identities for 18 clients, from logos to full style guides
  • Created social media templates that grew one client’s engagement by 60%
  • Led packaging design for a product line sold in 300 stores
  • Produced flyers, posters and web banners for local businesses
  • Redesigned a client’s website, raising time-on-page by 45%
  • Created 300+ social media posts a year for 6 brands
  • Designed a logo used on 2 million product packs
  • Presented concepts directly to clients and handled feedback
  • Built a brand template kit that cut design time by 30%

Tips for your graphic designer resume

  1. Always include a portfolio link in your header.
  2. Choose a stylish design, but keep the text easy to read.
  3. Mention the brands or kinds of clients you have worked with.

Questions people ask

Should a graphic designer use a creative resume?

A little style is good, but keep it readable. If you apply through job portals, pick a design marked “Job-portal friendly”.

What should a designer put on a resume?

Portfolio link, software, design specialities, clients or brands, and results your work achieved.

Can I add a photo?

Yes, our stylish designs like Teal Profile and Sidebar include a photo spot.
