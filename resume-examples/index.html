How to use a resume example

  1. Pick the job closest to yours and read why the example works.
  2. Tap “Use this example”. It opens in the builder with everything filled in.
  3. Replace every detail with your own — names, dates, companies and results. Never copy someone else’s achievements.
  4. Check it against the job advert with the Job match checker, then download your PDF.

All people and companies in these examples are made up.
