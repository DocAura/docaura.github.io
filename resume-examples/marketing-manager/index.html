Marketing Manager resume summary examples

Your summary is the first thing people read. Keep it to 2–3 lines: who you are, your experience, and what you are best at.

Digital marketer with 7 years of experience growing brands through SEO, paid ads and social media. Data-led, creative and comfortable managing budgets and teams.
Social media marketer with 4 years of experience growing brands on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. Built communities of 500K+ followers.
Performance marketing specialist managing $1M+ a year in Google and Meta ads for e-commerce brands, with a focus on lowering cost per sale.

Marketing Manager resume bullet points

Start with an action word and add a number to show the result. Copy these ideas and change them to match your own work:

  • Grew organic website traffic by 120% in 18 months through SEO and content marketing
  • Managed a £400,000 yearly Google Ads and Meta budget with a 4.2x return
  • Led a team of 3 marketers and 2 agencies
  • Launched email campaigns with a 32% open rate
  • Generated 3,500 qualified leads in one year through content and webinars
  • Cut cost per lead by 38% by restructuring Google Ads campaigns
  • Launched a new product line that sold out in 3 weeks
  • Grew the email list from 10,000 to 60,000 subscribers
  • Raised Instagram engagement by 70% with a new content plan

Tips for your marketing manager resume

  1. Show results with numbers: traffic, leads, revenue, ROAS, followers.
  2. List the tools you use (GA4, HubSpot, Semrush, Canva).
  3. Add a link to a portfolio or campaign examples if you have one.

Questions people ask

What should a marketing resume include?

Channels you manage, campaign results with numbers, budgets, tools, and any team leadership.

Can a marketing resume be creative?

Yes, a little colour or a sidebar is fine. Keep the text clear so ATS software can read it.

How do I show marketing results without exact numbers?

Use honest estimates or percentages (“about 30% more sign-ups”) and be ready to explain them.
