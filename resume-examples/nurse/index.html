Nurse resume example

Updated 20 October 2026 · Free to edit and download

Nursing employers check three things first: your registration, your clinical area and how you keep patients safe. This nurse resume example makes all three easy to see, with results like fewer medication errors.

Why this nurse resume works

  • Registration (NMC) and life-support certificates are clearly listed.
  • The ward type and size (28-bed surgical) gives real context.

  • A calm, clean design that is easy to read.

Nurse resume summary examples

Your summary is the first thing people read. Keep it to 2–3 lines: who you are, your experience, and what you are best at.

Caring registered nurse with 8 years of experience in busy medical and surgical wards. Calm under pressure, with strong patient care, medication and teamwork skills.
Compassionate registered nurse with 3 years of experience in emergency care. Skilled in triage, IV therapy and supporting patients and families in stressful moments.
Newly qualified nurse with strong placement experience in medical, surgical and community settings, looking for a first staff nurse role.

Skills to put on a nurse resume

Add the ones you really have — and use the same words as the job advert.

  • Patient assessment
  • Medication administration
  • IV therapy
  • Triage
  • Wound care
  • Infection control
  • Care planning
  • Electronic health records
  • Basic & Advanced Life Support
  • Patient education
  • Teamwork
  • Calm under pressure

Nurse resume bullet points

Start with an action word and add a number to show the result. Copy these ideas and change them to match your own work:

  • Lead a team of 6 nurses on a 28-bed surgical ward
  • Cut medication errors by 30% by introducing a double-check routine
  • Trained 15 newly qualified nurses on ward procedures and infection control
  • Cared for up to 8 patients per shift on an acute medical ward
  • Cared for up to 6 high-dependency patients per shift
  • Reduced patient falls on the ward by 25% by leading a safety project
  • Mentored 10 student nurses during placements
  • Kept patient records accurate and up to date in the EHR system
  • Took part in a hospital-wide sepsis awareness training programme

Tips for your nurse resume

  1. Put your registration number and licence type near the top or in Certifications.
  2. Name the specialities and wards you have worked in.
  3. Add clinical placements if you are newly qualified.

Questions people ask

Should a nurse resume include a licence number?

Yes, many employers like to see your registration body and number (for example NMC, NCLEX/RN licence).

How long should a nursing resume be?

One to two pages. Newly qualified nurses should aim for one page.

Which skills are most important for nurses?

Patient care, medication administration, infection control and record keeping — plus communication and teamwork.
