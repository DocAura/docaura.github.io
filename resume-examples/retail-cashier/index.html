Retail sales associate and cashier resume example

Store managers hire people who are friendly, reliable and good with money. This retail and cashier resume example shows sales results, accurate cash handling and the customer skills that matter on the shop floor.

Why this retail / cashier resume works

  • Shows sales targets beaten, not just duties.
  • Cash handling is backed up with “zero errors”.
  • Mentions flexibility — important for shift work.
  • A friendly design that still reads well on job portals.

Retail / Cashier resume summary examples

Your summary is the first thing people read. Keep it to 2–3 lines: who you are, your experience, and what you are best at.

Energetic retail sales associate with 3 years of experience in busy fashion and electronics stores. I love helping customers find the right product, and I am reliable with cash, stock and opening and closing.
Dependable cashier with 2 years of experience in a busy supermarket. Fast, accurate and always polite with customers.
First-time job seeker with volunteering and school event experience, ready to work weekends and evenings in a retail store.

Skills to put on a retail / cashier resume

Add the ones you really have — and use the same words as the job advert.

  • Customer service
  • Cash handling
  • POS systems
  • Stock replenishment
  • Visual merchandising
  • Upselling
  • Product knowledge
  • Teamwork
  • Reliability
  • Flexible hours

Retail / Cashier resume bullet points

Start with an action word and add a number to show the result. Copy these ideas and change them to match your own work:

  • Beat monthly sales targets for 14 of the last 16 months
  • Handle cash and card payments of $4,000+ a day with zero errors
  • Set up window and floor displays that raised accessory sales by 20%
  • Served 150+ customers a shift quickly and politely
  • Trained 5 new cashiers on the till and returns process
  • Received 20+ positive customer reviews by name
  • Helped run stocktake for a store with 5,000 products
  • Kept shelves full and tidy during peak holiday season
  • Signed up 200+ customers to the store loyalty card

Tips for your retail / cashier resume

  1. Mention your availability (weekends, evenings) in the summary.
  2. Show you are trusted with money: “balanced the till every shift”.
  3. Add any sales targets or customer compliments.

Questions people ask

What should I put on a cashier resume?

Cash handling, POS experience, customer service, speed and accuracy, and your availability.

I have never worked before. What do I write?

Use school, volunteering, clubs or helping in a family business. Focus on being reliable and good with people.
