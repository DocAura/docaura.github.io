Sales Executive resume summary examples

Your summary is the first thing people read. Keep it to 2–3 lines: who you are, your experience, and what you are best at.

Results-driven B2B sales professional with 5 years of experience closing software deals. Consistently above target, with a strong network in retail and logistics.
Field sales executive with 6 years of experience selling FMCG products to 300+ retailers. Consistently beat targets and opened 40 new accounts last year.
Inside sales representative with 2 years of experience in SaaS. Booked 30+ demos a month and closed $400K in first-year deals.

Skills to put on a sales executive resume

Add the ones you really have — and use the same words as the job advert.

  • B2B / B2C sales
  • Lead generation
  • Cold calling
  • Negotiation
  • Closing
  • CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot)
  • Pipeline management
  • Forecasting
  • Account management
  • Presentation
  • Relationship building
  • Upselling

Sales Executive resume bullet points

Start with an action word and add a number to show the result. Copy these ideas and change them to match your own work:

  • Closed $1.8M in new annual revenue in 2025, 132% of quota
  • Built a pipeline of 60+ qualified leads per quarter through cold calling and LinkedIn
  • Shortened the average sales cycle from 74 to 51 days
  • Booked 25 discovery meetings a month for 3 account executives
  • Achieved 118% of annual target, ranking #2 of 25 in the region
  • Opened 40 new retail accounts in one year
  • Grew revenue from key accounts by 35% through upselling
  • Won back 12 lost customers worth $200,000 a year
  • Cut the sales cycle by 20 days with a new demo process

Tips for your sales executive resume

  1. Lead with your best number — quota %, revenue, or ranking in the team.
  2. Use the job advert’s words: “account management”, “new business”, “B2B”.
  3. Show the size of deals and the type of customers you sell to.

Questions people ask

What should a sales resume include?

Targets and results (% of quota, revenue), the products you sold, the customers you sold to, and your CRM tools.

I have no sales experience. Can I still apply?

Yes. Show customer-facing work like retail, call centre or events, and any targets you met. See our guide for resumes with no experience.

Is a colourful design OK for sales jobs?

Yes. Sales is a people job and a little colour is fine. Choose a design marked “Job-portal friendly” so it still passes ATS.
