What makes a resume ATS-friendly?

  • Real text — our PDFs keep every word as selectable text, so software can read it.
  • Standard headings like “Experience”, “Education” and “Skills”.
  • No tables or text boxes for the main content.
  • Keywords from the job advert — use the Job match checker in our builder to see which ones you are missing.

Tip: after downloading, open the PDF and try to select the text. If you can, an ATS can read it too.

Questions people ask

Are these ATS templates really free?

Yes. Every template is free to use and download as a PDF — no sign-up, no watermark.

Can an ATS read a two-column resume?

Many modern systems can, but one-column layouts are the safest. All templates on this page are designed to be read correctly.

How do I check my resume against a job?

Open the resume builder, paste the job advert into the Job match checker, and it shows the skills you are missing.
