When to use a creative resume

  • Emailing your resume to a hiring manager or sharing it in person.
  • Design, media, beauty, hospitality, tourism and sales jobs.
  • Countries where a photo on the resume is normal.

Applying through a big job portal? Use an ATS-friendly template instead — you can switch designs in one tap without retyping anything.

Questions people ask

Can I add my photo?

Yes. Templates with a photo spot let you upload a photo — it stays on your device.

Are creative resumes bad for ATS?

Two-column designs can be harder for some older systems. For online portals, choose a design marked “Job-portal friendly”.

Can I switch to a simple design later?

Yes — your details stay the same; just pick another design.
