Why simple works

  • Recruiters skim. Clear headings and white space help them find what matters.
  • Simple designs print clearly and look the same on every device.
  • They are the safest choice for job portals and government applications.

How to make a simple resume stand out

Let the content shine: start bullets with strong action words, and add a number to show results. Our guide to resume action words has 120 ideas.

Questions people ask

What is the simplest resume format?

A one-column layout with your name and contact details, a short summary, experience, education and skills. The “Simple” template is exactly that.

Is a simple resume OK for senior jobs?

Yes. Many executives use simple, classic designs. Try Executive or Classic for a more formal look.

Can I print it?

Yes. Choose A4 or US Letter paper in the builder and print the PDF.
