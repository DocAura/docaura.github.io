    How to split a PDF

    1Choose your PDFEvery page is shown as a small picture so you can see what is inside.
    2Tap the pagesTap the pages to keep. Chosen pages turn pink with a tick.
    3SaveSave them as one new PDF, or each page as its own PDF.

    Questions people ask

    How do I take out some pages from a PDF?

    Choose your PDF, tap the pages you want to keep (they turn pink with a tick), then press “Save chosen pages”.

    How do I remove a page from a PDF?

    Tap “Choose all”, then tap the page you want to remove so it is no longer ticked, and save.

    Can I type page numbers instead?

    Yes. Type something like 1-3, 5 in the page numbers box and the pages are chosen for you.

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. Your PDF is split inside your own browser.
