Questions people ask

How many cm in an inch?

1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly.

How do I convert °C to °F?

°F = °C × 9/5 + 32. So 37 °C = 98.6 °F.

How many kg is a pound?

1 lb = 0.45359237 kg, so 1 kg ≈ 2.2046 lb.

Acre to hectare?

1 acre = 0.404686 hectare; 1 hectare ≈ 2.471 acres.

How many miles is 1 km?

1 km = 0.621371 miles, and 1 mile = 1.609344 km.

How many litres are in a gallon?

1 US gallon = 3.78541 litres. 1 UK (imperial) gallon = 4.54609 litres.

How do I convert square feet to square metres?

1 sq ft = 0.092903 sq m, and 1 sq m ≈ 10.764 sq ft.
